# Examples

Use the [quickstart](../QUICKSTART.md#3b-no-filemaker-file-start-from-nothing) to apply an app's design folder, or browse the [documentation](../docs/README.md).

## Apps

- [First app](first-app/README.md) — A small contacts book built from an empty database.
- [Invoices](invoices/README.md) — Customers, products, invoices and line items with related calculations and scripts.
- [Support desk](support-desk/README.md) — A ticket list for trying AI-assisted triage under your access rules.
- [CRM](crm/README.md) — Companies, contacts and deals with a report of open opportunities.
- [Inventory](inventory/README.md) — A stock movement ledger with receiving, selling and reorder scripts.
- [Time tracking](time-tracking/README.md) — Project hours with related calculations and a summary report.

## Plug-ins

- [Rust text tools](plugins/rust-text-tools) — A WebAssembly plug-in with slug, word-count and addition functions; see [Writing a plug-in](../docs/writing-a-plugin.md#in-webassembly) for the interface.
